# Source note: U.S. Climate Normals (NOAA)

- **Link:** https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals
- **Published by:** NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI). The current set covers 1991 to 2020, first released in May 2021.
- **Read:** 2026-11-13

This note is a summary in our own words. It is never edited after it's added; corrections go on the wiki pages.

## What it says that matters to the garden

- Climate normals are 30-year averages and statistics of weather for almost 15,000 U.S. weather stations. NOAA describes them as a guide to typical conditions, used for example by farmers choosing what to plant.
- NOAA recalculates them every 10 years. The next set would cover 2001 to 2030.
- You can search by station, with a quick-access tool or a fuller data tool, or download files.
- NOAA's description of the earlier (1981 to 2010) set lists "agricultural normals" among its products, including the probabilities of frost and freeze dates and the length of the growing season.

## What we didn't confirm

Whether the quick-access tool shows frost dates directly, or whether you need the full data tool or the download files. Check this when the group looks up its own station.
