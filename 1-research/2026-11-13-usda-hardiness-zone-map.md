# Source note: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map

- **Link:** https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/
- **Published by:** U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. The current map is the 2023 edition.
- **Read:** 2026-11-13

This note is a summary in our own words. It is never edited after it's added; corrections go on the wiki pages.

## What it says that matters to the garden

- The map tells you which **perennial** plants (ones that live through winter) are likely to thrive in a place.
- Zones are based on the **average coldest temperature of the winter** at a location, in 10°F zones and 5°F half-zones.
- You can look up a zone by ZIP code on the map's page.

## What it doesn't say

The zone is about winter cold, not about **when** the last spring frost happens, so it doesn't answer "when can we plant?" on its own.
