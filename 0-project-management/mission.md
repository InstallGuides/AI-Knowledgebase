# Mission: the community garden wiki

## What this wiki is for

A neighborhood group wants to start a community garden. This wiki collects what the group learns while planning it: finding a plot, the budget, water, volunteers, and when to plant. Each page says what is known, where that came from, and what is still unknown, so anyone in the group (or their AI assistant) can pick up where the last person stopped.

## Who it serves

The people planning the garden. None of them are experts, so pages use plain words.

## What the group knows so far

These are the group's own plans and facts, from its first planning notes. Wiki pages link here when they mention them; claims about the outside world link a source note in `1-research/` instead.

- Sam can get compost.
- The starting budget is maybe $400, for seeds, a few tools and a water hookup.
- The group would like to plant by spring, so decisions need to happen in the next few weeks.
- The library might host a sign-up table on a Saturday.
- Maya knows someone at the Parks Department.
- Still to do: make a flyer, start an email list, and find about five volunteers.
- The city may have a program that offers plots. Nobody has checked yet.

## What it leaves out

- Legal advice. Leases and insurance are noted as questions to ask, not answered.
- Facts about a particular town. The group's location isn't recorded, so local facts (such as the last-frost date) stay as open questions until it is.
- Copied text from sources. Source notes are written in our own words, with a link.
