# Water

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

The group's starting budget includes a water hookup ([mission](../../0-project-management/mission.md)).

## What to check

- **Is there water on or near the site?** It's one of the first questions for any site ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)).
- **Call the local water provider** with the site's address, and ask whether there's a water meter and whether they will assess the site ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).
- **Meter size:** about half an inch to one inch, depending on the garden's size ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).

## Why it matters for the budget

A site that had water service before is cheaper to connect. A site that never had it may need a new line and meter, which can be expensive ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)). See [budget](budget.md).

## Rules to agree on

Whether water can be left running unattended belongs in the gardener guidelines ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)).
