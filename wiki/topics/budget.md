# Budget

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13
**Review by:** 2027-11-13

## What the group has planned

About $400 to start, for seeds, a few tools and a water hookup. Sam can get compost ([mission](../../0-project-management/mission.md)).

## What the sources say it costs

- A garden with just the basics typically costs **$2,500 to $5,000** to start, and groups often build it in phases ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md), published 2023). The guide's basics include things like fencing and a water connection.
- **Water can be the largest cost.** A site that never had water service may need a new line and meter ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)). See [water](water.md).
- Other costs to plan for: tools, seeds, mulch, a tool shed, fencing, mowing and **liability insurance** ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)).

## The gap

The group's $400 is far below the sources' $2,500 to $5,000. It could still work if the site already has water and a fence, and if tools and materials are donated. Otherwise, plan a first phase that fits $400 and raise the rest.

## Ways to raise money

- **A gardener fee,** possibly on a sliding scale ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)).
- **Donations and sponsors:** local businesses, nonprofits or city departments ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)). Neighbors give more readily when the group shows a specific list of what it needs.
