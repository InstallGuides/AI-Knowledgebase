# Finding a plot

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

The group needs land, and the plan is to plant by spring ([mission](../../0-project-management/mission.md)).

## What makes a good site

- **Sun:** at least six hours of direct sun a day for vegetables ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)); six to eight hours ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)). The two sources differ slightly; aim for more than six.
- **Water** on or near the site. See [water](water.md).
- **Fairly flat,** without large rubble, and close enough for gardeners to walk or drive a short way ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md), [Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).
- **Visible from the street,** which makes it safer and draws neighbors' support, and reachable by a truck ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)).
- **Clean soil.** Ask how the land was used before. Old city lots can have contaminated soil, so test it for heavy metals before signing anything ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md)). The county Extension office can provide a soil test ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).

## How to get permission

1. List **three possible sites**; starting with three makes success more likely ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).
2. Find each owner through the **county tax assessor** ([MU Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md), [Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)). Using land without permission is illegal.
3. Ask the owner for a lease. Groups often pay **$1 a year**, for **at least three years**, with a "hold harmless" waiver, and may need liability insurance ([Alabama Extension](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md)).
