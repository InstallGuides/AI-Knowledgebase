# Source: U.S. Climate Normals (NOAA)

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

**Note:** [1-research/2026-11-13-noaa-climate-normals.md](../../1-research/2026-11-13-noaa-climate-normals.md) · **Link:** https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/us-climate-normals · **Read:** 2026-11-13

The U.S. government's 30-year weather statistics for almost 15,000 stations, currently covering 1991 to 2020 and recalculated every 10 years.

## What the wiki takes from it

- Where to find the odds of the last spring frost, by weather station.
- That the numbers are averages over 30 years, not a forecast.
