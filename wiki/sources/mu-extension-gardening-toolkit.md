# Source: Community Gardening Toolkit (University of Missouri Extension)

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

**Note:** [1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md](../../1-research/2026-11-13-mu-extension-gardening-toolkit.md) · **Link:** https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/mp906 · **Read:** 2026-11-13

A step-by-step guide from a university extension service, revised in March 2026. Strongest on running the group: the first meeting, choosing a site, and gardener rules.

## What the wiki takes from it

- How to judge a site: sun, water, size, past use, soil testing, finding the owner.
- What to line up: tools, compost, fencing, insurance, local help.
- Gardener guidelines, and the work of keeping a garden going.
