# Source: A Guide to Starting a Community Garden (Alabama Cooperative Extension System)

**Last updated:** 2026-11-13

**Note:** [1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md](../../1-research/2026-11-13-alabama-extension-garden-guide.md) · **Link:** https://www.aces.edu/blog/topics/business-community-urban/a-guide-to-starting-a-community-garden/ · **Read:** 2026-11-13

A start-up guide from a university extension service, published in 2023. Strongest on practical numbers: group size, lease terms, water meters and start-up cost.

## What the wiki takes from it

- About five families as a minimum, and a committee with three officers.
- Six to eight hours of sun; three candidate sites; a $1-a-year lease for at least three years.
- Checking water with the local provider, and meter sizes.
- A typical start-up cost of $2,500 to $5,000. It's a 2023 figure from Alabama, so check local prices.
