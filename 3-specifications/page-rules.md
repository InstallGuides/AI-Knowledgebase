# Page rules for the wiki

Read this before writing anything in `wiki/`.

## The three kinds of page

| Kind | Folder | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Topic | `wiki/topics/` | What the group knows about one part of the plan, such as water or the budget. |
| Question | `wiki/questions/` | One open question: what's known, what isn't, and how to find out. When it's answered, the answer moves to a topic page and the question page says so. |
| Source | `wiki/sources/` | One page per source note in `1-research/`: what the wiki takes from it, and which pages use it. Same name as the note, without the date. |

`wiki/index.md` lists every page. `wiki/log.md` gets one line, newest first, for every change.

## Names

Lowercase words joined by hyphens, such as `finding-a-plot.md`. A page keeps its name once other pages link to it.

## Evidence rules

1. **Every claim about the world links to its source note** in `1-research/`. A claim with no source doesn't go in.
2. **The group's own plans and facts link to** `0-project-management/mission.md` instead.
3. **When sources disagree, say so** and give both, each with its link. Don't pick one quietly.
4. **Write "unknown" rather than guess.** List unknowns under "Still unknown" on the page.
5. **Date anything that changes,** such as prices, rules or programs: write when the source was read.
6. **Pages with facts that change** carry a `**Review by:**` date, a year or less away. The `check-the-wiki` skill reports pages past that date.
7. **No copied text from sources.** Write in your own words.

## Page layout

A title, then `**Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD` (and `**Review by:**` if rule 6 applies), then the content, then a "Still unknown" list if there is anything unknown.
