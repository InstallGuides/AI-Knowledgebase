# Ask the wiki

1. **Start at** `wiki/index.md`, and read the pages that bear on the question, plus the source notes they link when you need the detail.
2. **Answer from the wiki only.** Give each point with a link to the page it came from. Keep the sources' disagreements visible.
3. **Say what's missing.** If the wiki doesn't answer the question, or the answer is on a page past its review date, say so plainly.
4. **Offer next steps:** a source worth adding (the `add-a-source` skill), or a new question page in `wiki/questions/` if the gap matters.

Don't fill gaps from your own general knowledge without saying so. If you add something from outside the wiki, label it as unchecked, and suggest finding a source for it.
